Privacy Policy

How Shelfa handles information and protects your privacy.

Effective date: August 30, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Shelfa handles information when you use the app and related services. Shelfa is designed for private communication within closed communities. It does not provide public account search or public user discovery.

2. Information We May Process

To provide the service, Shelfa may process information you provide or generate while using the app, including:

  • Your chosen nickname and device-related identifiers needed to operate the service.
  • Contact requests, contact relationships, blacklist information, and reports.
  • Messages and content you choose to send, such as text, voice messages, photos, and videos.
  • Technical information reasonably necessary for app operation, troubleshooting, security, and delivery of notifications.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide and maintain Shelfa, deliver communications and notifications, process contact requests, support blocking and reporting features, investigate technical problems, protect users, and enforce applicable rules.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not make your profile publicly searchable. Information may be shared with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, or when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to investigate misuse. Content you send is shared with the intended recipient as part of the service.

5. User-Generated Content and Safety

Shelfa provides contact approval, blacklisting, and reporting tools. Users can report inappropriate content or behavior. Reports may be reviewed and appropriate action may be taken when content or behavior violates our rules or applicable law.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information handled by the service. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid sending highly sensitive information through the app.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our terms. You may contact support to ask about deletion of information associated with your use of Shelfa, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Children

Shelfa is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to consent to use of an online service in their jurisdiction without appropriate parental or guardian authorization.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or legal requirements change. The effective date shown on this page indicates the latest version.
